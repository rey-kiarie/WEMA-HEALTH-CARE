Nutrition Safety & Healthy Living

Based on WHO & Kenya National Nutrition Action Plan 2025–2030 guidelines

Balanced Diet Guidelines

  • • Eat a variety of foods from all food groups daily
  • • Include fruits & vegetables (5 servings/day)
  • • Choose whole grains over refined grains
  • • Limit sugar to <6 teaspoons/day (WHO)
  • • Reduce salt intake to <5g/day
  • • Drink at least 8 glasses of clean water daily

Food Safety Practices

  • • Wash hands before handling food
  • • Cook food thoroughly (70°C+ internal)
  • • Store food at proper temperatures
  • • Avoid cross-contamination
  • • Use safe, clean water for cooking
  • • Check expiry dates on all products

Maternal & Child Nutrition

  • • Exclusive breastfeeding for first 6 months
  • • Introduce complementary foods at 6 months
  • • Continue breastfeeding up to 2 years
  • • Iron & folic acid supplements during pregnancy
  • • Vitamin A supplementation for children 6-59 months
  • • Monitor growth using MUAC & weight charts

Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH)

  • • Use treated/boiled water for drinking
  • • Practice handwashing with soap at critical times
  • • Use improved sanitation facilities
  • • Proper waste disposal & management
  • • Keep living environment clean
  • • Prevent waterborne diseases (cholera, typhoid)

Mental Health & Wellbeing

  • • Get 7-9 hours of sleep daily
  • • Exercise at least 150 min/week (WHO)
  • • Manage stress through relaxation techniques
  • • Maintain social connections
  • • Seek professional help when needed
  • • Limit alcohol & avoid substance abuse

NCD Prevention Diet

  • • Reduce saturated & trans fats
  • • Increase dietary fibre intake
  • • Limit processed & red meat
  • • Choose healthy cooking oils
  • • Maintain healthy body weight (BMI 18.5-24.9)
  • • Regular blood pressure & sugar monitoring

Disease Prevention Procedures

Evidence-based prevention strategies — WHO & Kenya MOH 2025/2026

Malaria Prevention

  • ✅ Sleep under insecticide-treated nets (ITNs) every night
  • ✅ Use indoor residual spraying (IRS)
  • ✅ Eliminate standing water around homes
  • ✅ Wear long-sleeved clothing at dusk/dawn
  • ✅ Use DEET-based mosquito repellent
  • ✅ Seek immediate testing if fever develops
  • ✅ Intermittent Preventive Treatment for pregnant women (IPTp)

HIV/AIDS Prevention

  • ✅ ABC: Abstain, Be faithful, use Condoms
  • ✅ Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP) for high-risk individuals
  • ✅ Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP) within 72 hours
  • ✅ Regular HIV testing & counselling (HTC)
  • ✅ Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission (PMTCT)
  • ✅ Safe blood transfusion practices
  • ✅ Antiretroviral Therapy (ART) for positive individuals (U=U)

Tuberculosis Prevention

  • ✅ BCG vaccination for newborns
  • ✅ Ensure good ventilation in living spaces
  • ✅ Cover mouth when coughing/sneezing
  • ✅ Complete full DOTS treatment course (6 months)
  • ✅ Screen close contacts of TB patients
  • ✅ Maintain strong immune system through nutrition
  • ✅ Regular chest X-rays for high-risk individuals

NCDs Prevention

  • ✅ Regular physical activity (150 min/week moderate)
  • ✅ Maintain healthy BMI (18.5-24.9)
  • ✅ Avoid tobacco in all forms
  • ✅ Limit alcohol consumption
  • ✅ Regular screening: BP, blood sugar, cholesterol
  • ✅ Cancer screening: cervical, breast, prostate
  • ✅ HPV vaccination for cervical cancer prevention
